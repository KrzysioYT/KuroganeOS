# Contributing to KuroganeOS

Thank you for considering a contribution to KuroganeOS.

KuroganeOS is developed in public, but current revisions are distributed under
the KuroganeOS Source-Available License 1.0 (KSAL-1.0), not under an
OSI-approved Open Source license.

## Before contributing

Please:

1. read `LICENSE`;
2. read `docs/LICENSING.md`;
3. read `CLA.md`;
4. make sure your contribution is your own work or that you have sufficient
   rights to submit it;
5. do not submit code copied from another project unless its license is
   explicitly compatible and the required notices are included.

## Contributor License Agreement

The project may require acceptance of `CLA.md` before a contribution can be
merged.

For contributions where CLA acceptance is required, include the following
statement in the pull request description:

```text
I have read and agree to the KuroganeOS Contributor License Agreement (CLA).
```

The project owner may request stronger evidence of acceptance for significant
or legally sensitive contributions.

## Copyright

You keep the copyright in your contribution unless a separate written
agreement states otherwise.

By accepting the CLA, you grant the KuroganeOS project owner the rights
described in that agreement, including rights needed to distribute, modify,
sublicense and relicense your contribution as part of KuroganeOS.

## Third-party code

If your contribution contains third-party material, clearly identify:

- the source;
- the copyright holder;
- the applicable license;
- any attribution or notice requirements.

Do not submit code whose license prevents the project from distributing it
under the intended KuroganeOS licensing model.

## Project discretion

Submitting a contribution does not guarantee that it will be merged.

The project owner may reject, modify, defer or request changes to a
contribution for technical, architectural, security, legal or licensing
reasons.
