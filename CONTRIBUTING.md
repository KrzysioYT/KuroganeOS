# Contributing to KuroganeOS

Thank you for considering a contribution to KuroganeOS.

KuroganeOS is developed in public, but current revisions are distributed under
the **KuroganeOS Source-Available License 2.0 (KSAL-2.0)**, not under an
OSI-approved Open Source license.

## Before contributing

Please:

1. read `LICENSE`;
2. read `docs/LICENSING.md`;
3. read `CLA.md`;
4. make sure Your contribution is Your own work or that You have sufficient
   rights to submit it;
5. disclose all third-party material, licenses, attribution requirements, and
   source-availability obligations included in the contribution;
6. do not submit confidential, proprietary, leaked, or otherwise unauthorized
   material.

## Contributor License Agreement — required before merge

Acceptance of `CLA.md` is required before a contribution can be incorporated
into the official KuroganeOS repository, unless the project owner approves a
separate written contribution agreement.

Include the following statement in the pull request description when the
project's contribution workflow asks for CLA acceptance:

```text
I have read and agree to the KuroganeOS Contributor License Agreement (CLA).
```

The project owner may require acceptance through a pull-request checkbox,
signed commit, account-linked workflow, electronic signature, or another
reasonably verifiable method. Significant or legally sensitive contributions
may require separately signed acceptance.

A contribution may be reviewed before CLA acceptance, but it must not be merged
until the required agreement is in place.

## Copyright

You keep the copyright in Your contribution unless a separate written agreement
states otherwise.

By accepting the CLA, You grant the KuroganeOS project owner the rights
described in that agreement, including rights needed to use, modify,
distribute, sublicense, relicense, dual-license, and commercially license the
accepted contribution as part of KuroganeOS or related offerings.

## Independent implementations and compatibility work

Compatibility work should be written independently. Do not copy KuroganeOS
source into another project and describe the result as an independent
implementation.

If a contribution is derived from interoperability research, clearly document
its origin and any third-party constraints. Keeping development notes may help
show how independently written compatibility code was produced.

## Third-party code

If Your contribution contains third-party material, clearly identify:

- the source;
- the copyright holder;
- the applicable license;
- any attribution or notice requirements;
- any source-distribution or reciprocal-license obligations.

Do not submit code whose terms prevent the project owner from exercising the
rights required by the KuroganeOS licensing model and CLA.

## Project discretion

Submitting a contribution does not guarantee that it will be merged.

The project owner may reject, modify, defer, or request changes to a
contribution for technical, architectural, security, legal, licensing, project
governance, or other reasons.
