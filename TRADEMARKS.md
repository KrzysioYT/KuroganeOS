# KuroganeOS Trademark and Branding Policy

This document governs use of the KuroganeOS project name, logos and official
release identity to the extent those identifiers are protected by applicable
law.

## Allowed without separate permission

You may use the name "KuroganeOS" solely to truthfully refer to the official
project, for example:

- "compatible with KuroganeOS";
- "a review of KuroganeOS";
- "a patch proposed for KuroganeOS";
- "based on an historical MIT-licensed revision of KuroganeOS",

provided that the use does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation or
official status.

## Permission required

Without prior written permission from the project owner, do not:

- present a modified build as an official KuroganeOS release;
- use the KuroganeOS logo as the primary branding of another project;
- name a derivative distribution "KuroganeOS" or a confusingly similar name;
- use project branding to imply endorsement;
- sell goods or services using KuroganeOS branding as their primary identity.

## Forks and modifications

A technical fork created for contribution purposes may retain repository
references necessary for Git history and attribution.

If separate permission is granted to publicly distribute a derivative product,
the permission may require a different name, logo and visual identity.

KSAL-1.0 grants copyright permissions only within its stated scope and does not
grant a trademark or branding license.
