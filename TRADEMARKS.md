# KuroganeOS Trademark and Branding Policy

This document governs use of the KuroganeOS project name, logos, visual
identity, and official-release identity to the extent those identifiers are
protected by applicable law.

## Allowed without separate permission

You may use the name "KuroganeOS" solely to truthfully identify or refer to the
official project, for example:

- "compatible with KuroganeOS";
- "a review of KuroganeOS";
- "a tutorial for KuroganeOS";
- "a patch proposed for KuroganeOS";
- "an independent implementation compatible with KuroganeOS";
- "based on a historical MIT-licensed revision of KuroganeOS".

Reviews, videos, livestreams, screenshots, benchmarks, tutorials, news coverage,
and similar media may use the name KuroganeOS truthfully, including in
monetized coverage permitted by KSAL-2.0, provided the use does not imply
sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation, or official status.

## Permission required

Without prior written permission from the project owner, do not:

- present a modified build as an official KuroganeOS release;
- use the KuroganeOS logo or visual identity as the primary branding of another
  project, product, distribution, or service;
- name a derivative distribution "KuroganeOS" or use a confusingly similar
  name in a manner that implies official origin;
- use project branding to imply sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation, or
  authorization;
- sell goods or services using KuroganeOS branding as their primary identity;
- register or use a domain name, package identity, store listing, or social
  account in a manner that falsely presents it as an official KuroganeOS
  channel.

## Forks and modifications

A technical fork created for contribution purposes may retain repository
references necessary for Git history, platform functionality, and attribution.

If separate permission is granted to publicly distribute a derivative product,
the permission may require a different name, logo, visual identity, and release
channel.

Independent implementations may truthfully state compatibility with KuroganeOS
but must not present themselves as KuroganeOS or as an official KuroganeOS
release.

## No trademark license in KSAL

KSAL-2.0 grants no trademark or branding license except the limited truthful
nominative references described by the license and this policy.

Copyright permission, interoperability permission, and trademark permission are
separate matters.

## Reservation

All rights in KuroganeOS branding not expressly granted here are reserved,
subject to rights that cannot be restricted under applicable law.
