# KuroganeOS Licensing

## Current licensing model

Current KuroganeOS revisions are distributed under the **KuroganeOS
Source-Available License 2.0 (KSAL-2.0)** unless a file explicitly states
otherwise.

KSAL-2.0 is a **source-available** license. It is not an OSI-approved Open
Source license.

The controlling legal text is the repository root [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE)
file. This document is a practical summary and does not replace that text.

## What KSAL-2.0 allows by default

Without requesting additional permission, KSAL-2.0 allows:

- viewing and studying the source code;
- cloning the repository;
- private non-commercial execution;
- private non-commercial modifications;
- personal, educational, academic-research, charitable, testing, and other
  non-commercial use within the license scope;
- use of the public source-hosting platform's normal fork workflow for
  evaluating the project and proposing changes back to the official repository;
- short attributed source quotations for commentary, criticism, research,
  security discussion, or teaching;
- reviews, tutorials, screenshots, videos, livestreams, benchmarks, news
  reporting, and similar media coverage, including monetized coverage, so long
  as that coverage does not redistribute KuroganeOS or a Modified Work beyond
  incidental excerpts reasonably necessary for the coverage;
- independently created compatible implementations that do not reproduce
  copyright-protected expression from KuroganeOS, subject to applicable patent,
  trademark, and other law.

## What requires written permission

Unless a separate written permission or agreement says otherwise, KSAL-2.0
does **not** permit:

- commercial use of KuroganeOS itself or a Modified Work, except the narrow
  Media and Commentary Use expressly permitted by the license;
- internal business deployment by or on behalf of a for-profit entity;
- sale, monetization, sublicensing, or relicensing of KuroganeOS itself;
- public redistribution of source or modified source outside the limited
  repository-fork and quotation/media permissions;
- redistribution of unofficial binaries, ISOs, installers, packaged releases,
  or modified releases;
- rebranding KuroganeOS as another official or endorsed product;
- copying, adapting, translating, porting, or reusing copyright-protected
  KuroganeOS expression in another operating system, application, library,
  firmware, product, or service;
- offering KuroganeOS or a Modified Work as a hosted, managed, cloud, SaaS,
  PaaS, appliance, or similar service.

Contact the project owner for rights outside KSAL-2.0's default scope.

## Compatibility and independent implementations

KSAL-2.0 deliberately distinguishes copying KuroganeOS code from independently
implementing compatible functionality.

The license does not claim copyright ownership over ideas, principles,
functionality, APIs, ABIs, syscall numbers, protocol constants, data formats,
or other elements to the extent applicable law does not protect them by
copyright.

An independent implementation must be independently created and must not copy,
adapt, translate, port, or otherwise reproduce copyright-protected expression
from KuroganeOS except where the license or mandatory law permits it.

There is **no percentage or line-count safe harbor**. A small copied fragment
can still matter if it is protected expression, while independently written
compatible code is not converted into KuroganeOS code merely because it
implements the same interface or behavior.

Nothing in KSAL-2.0 attempts to remove mandatory rights that applicable law
provides for observation, study, testing, interoperability, decompilation, or
other acts that cannot legally be excluded by contract.

The project owner may separately grant rights for:

- commercial deployment;
- commercial integration;

- hosted or managed use;
- OEM or appliance use;

- derivative products;
- other negotiated uses.

A commercial permission applies only to the person or entity, versions, scope,
purpose, and duration stated in the written agreement.

## Contributions and required CLA

Contributions are welcome, but **CLA acceptance is required before a
Contribution is incorporated into the official KuroganeOS repository** unless
the project owner accepts another written contribution agreement.

Contributors retain copyright in their Contributions unless a separate written
assignment says otherwise. The CLA grants the project owner the rights needed
to modify, distribute, sublicense, relicense, dual-license, and commercially
license accepted Contributions as part of KuroganeOS or related offerings.

Contributors must read:

- [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md)
- [`CLA.md`](../CLA.md)

The standard acceptance statement is:

```text
I have read and agree to the KuroganeOS Contributor License Agreement (CLA).
```

The project owner may require a more verifiable acceptance method for
significant or legally sensitive Contributions.

## Third-party components

KSAL-2.0 applies only to material for which the KuroganeOS licensor has
authority to grant KSAL rights.

Third-party files, libraries, specifications, headers, firmware, tools, assets,
or other materials remain governed by their own licenses and notices. If a file
or component carries its own license notice, that notice controls for that file
or component.

## Public repository hosting

A public repository may be viewable and forkable through platform functionality
under the hosting provider's own terms. That platform-level permission is
separate from the copyright permissions granted by KSAL-2.0.

Keeping the source repository public therefore does not make KuroganeOS an
OSI-approved Open Source project.

stated scope.
